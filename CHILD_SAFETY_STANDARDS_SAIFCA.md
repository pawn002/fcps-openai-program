# Safe AI For Children Alliance (SAIFCA) Research

## Overview

The Safe AI for Children Alliance (SAIFCA) is a UK-based non-profit organization (Community Interest Company) dedicated to protecting children from AI-related risks. Founded by Tara Steele, the organization brings together expertise from AI governance, child protection, education, law, and public policy.

**Legal Status**: Community Interest Company (CIC), UK Company Number 16284722

---

## Mission

> "Protect children from the risks posed by artificial intelligence, inspire a future generation equipped to ensure advanced AI systems align with human well-being, and contribute meaningfully to societal efforts addressing AI challenges."

SAIFCA works to ensure that:
- Children's rights, safety, and well-being are embedded in AI policy, regulation, and standards
- Safeguards keep pace with technological change
- The next generation is prepared for an AI-driven future

---

## Vision

**"Building a safe AI future for children"**

The alliance envisions a world where:
- Children are protected from AI risks while being prepared to thrive in an AI-driven world
- Advanced AI systems are developed and governed with care
- Catastrophic risks that could impact future generations are prevented

## Core Values & Principles

SAIFCA operates on several foundational principles:

### Child-Centered Approach
- Children deserve to grow up in a world that is safe today and prepared for the future they will inherit

### Collaborative Action
- Uniting experts, educators, and advocates to foster collective action

- Strengthening partnerships that advance ethical AI innovation

### Practical Impact
- Raising awareness and influencing decision-making
- Supporting practical action that places children's safety at the center

- Responsible development and use of AI for humanity's benefit

## The Global Non-Negotiables Framework

SAIFCA's flagship policy framework establishes universal baseline protections for children in the age of AI.

### The Three Non-Negotiables

These represent "red lines" — protections so fundamental they form the minimum baseline of civilization in the AI age:

| Non-Negotiable | Description |
|----------------|-------------|
| **1. No Sexualized Content** | AI must never create fake sexualized images of children. Systems capable of this should not be available. |
| **2. No Emotional Manipulation** | AI must never be designed to make children emotionally dependent or exploit children's emotional needs. |
| **3. No Self-Harm Encouragement** | AI must never encourage children to harm themselves, including promoting eating disorders, violence, or other dangerous behaviors. |

### Selection Criteria

These non-negotiables were chosen because they:
- Address documented, current harms
- Are technically achievable
- Can be implemented quickly
- Cover three of the most severe immediate risks
- Leave room for beneficial AI uses

### Four-Layer Enforcement Structure

The framework implements protections through a comprehensive architecture:

| Layer | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Prevention** | Stopping harmful AI systems before deployment |
| **Detection** | Identifying violations as they occur |
| **Enforcement** | Taking action against violators |
| **Accountability** | Establishing responsibility mechanisms |

### Key Characteristics
- **Jurisdiction-agnostic**: Adaptable across different legal systems while preserving core protections
- **Immediately actionable**: Not aspirational guidelines but protections to be embedded in law, design, and culture now
- **Globally applicable**: Designed for worldwide implementation

## Policy Recommendations

SAIFCA calls for governments and technology companies to:

1. **Mandatory Testing**: Require independent testing and transparency before powerful models are released
2. **Safety by Design**: Embed child-safety requirements in AI design from the start
3. **Age Verification**: Enforce age checks and clear labeling for AI-generated material
4. **Public Education**: Support education so no child is left behind in understanding AI technology

## Perspective on AI Risks

### Immediate Risks (Current Focus)
- Inappropriate content from AI systems
- Psychological manipulation and exploitation
- AI-generated child sexual abuse material
- Biased algorithms affecting children
- Erosion of democratic trust

### Long-Term Perspective
SAIFCA acknowledges that "advanced AI systems could pose catastrophic global risks" and supports broader awareness efforts. However, they maintain focus on present-day dangers, viewing near-term protection as groundwork for preventing larger-scale AI failures.

> "By focusing on children today, we also help build the foundation for a world where advanced AI systems are developed and governed with care, preventing catastrophic risks that could impact future generations."

## Relevance to FCPS-OpenAI Evaluation

SAIFCA's framework provides valuable principles for evaluating AI partnerships in education:

| SAIFCA Principle | Application to FCPS Partnership |
|------------------|--------------------------------|
| Child-centered approach | Ensure student interests remain primary |
| Safety by design | Verify AI tools have built-in protections |
| Transparency | Demand clear disclosure of AI capabilities and limitations |
| Prevention focus | Assess risks before full deployment |
| Accountability mechanisms | Establish clear responsibility for any harms |
| No emotional manipulation | Monitor for AI features that could create dependency |

SAIFCA's Four-Layer Enforcement Structure (Prevention → Detection → Enforcement → Accountability) aligns well with our evaluation framework's approach to identifying and responding to risks at multiple levels.
